Privacy notice

How we use your details

Last updated 9 November 2026.

Who we are

Keystone is run by Bilal Latif Khan, a sole trader based in Slough, England. We are the controller of the details you give us on this website. Contact: keystoneconsultancy.co@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you ask for an estimate: your name, business email, company name, annual revenue band, the services you are interested in and whether you ticked the box to hear from us. To stop abuse we also keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address and the time of your request. We may look up public information about your business (for example its website, Companies House record and public reviews) to understand whether we can help.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To send your code and show your estimate, and to reply if you contact us: to take steps you asked for before a possible contract.
  • To decide whether and how to follow up (for example whether the Finance Ops Audit is free or £100 for you) and to prevent spam and abuse: our legitimate interests in running our business. You can object at any time.
  • To send you tips and offers by email: only if you ticked the box (your consent). The box is never ticked for you. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

How long we keep it

Verification codes expire after 15 minutes and request records are deleted after 30 days. If you do not become a client, we delete your details within 24 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires (normally 6 years for accounting records).

Who else handles it

We use trusted providers to run Keystone: n8n (automation, EU), Airtable (records), Resend (sending emails), Anthropic (AI used to research and score enquiries) and Google (email and calendar). Some are based in the USA; where your details leave the UK they are protected by the UK's approved safeguards (the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum). We never sell your details.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, ask us to restrict or stop using them, object to how we use them, and withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Email us and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
